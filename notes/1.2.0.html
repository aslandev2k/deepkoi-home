Deepkoi 1.2.0

  • A break, an interruption and Time's up each have their own color on the sticky bar: amber, red and green.
  • The sticky bar stays still when the "…" button appears, and the menu bar item keeps its width while the time counts down.
  • Music: see a track's progress and move through it, pick Repeat, In order or Shuffle, and carry on where it stopped in the next session.
  • Hold a stepper's - or + to keep counting, from minimum to maximum in about 3 seconds.
  • A new About group in Settings shows the version and has Check for Updates.
  • Fix: a new task moved to another epic before its title is typed now shows its title in the list, with only the open task highlighted.
