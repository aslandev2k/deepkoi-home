Deepkoi 1.3.0

  • Start a session on any task that is not done: the popup lists the epic chip's tasks first, then the other epics, most recently touched first.
  • Find a task by a few letters or with a typo. Tab opens the epic list.
  • When the sticky bar sits idle for 15 minutes while you use the Mac, it nudges you with a short line. Change or turn it off in Settings > Focus.
  • A new Session done sound plays when a session's note card closes.
  • Every button and select menu now shares one glass look.
  • A task with no sessions no longer shows "No sessions yet".
  • Your data moves to a new version, backed up first.
