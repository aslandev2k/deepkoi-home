Deepkoi 1.4.0

  • Open a task as a sticky note: a small window with its title and content that you can edit, pin above every app, collapse and resize. Use File > Open as Sticky Note (Shift-Command-K) or a task's right-click menu.
  • Settings > Sticky notes can reopen your notes, where you left them, when the app opens.
  • Right-click an epic in the Tasks page to edit or delete it, and a task to start a session, move it, mark it done, open it as a sticky note or delete it.
  • A new switch in Settings > Focus, Hide the app when a session starts, sends the main window to the Dock while you focus. It is on by default.
