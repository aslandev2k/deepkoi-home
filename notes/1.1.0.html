Deepkoi 1.1.0

  • Check for Updates in the Deepkoi menu (Option-Command-U). Deepkoi connects only when you click it.
  • Your data is backed up before a new version changes it, and the newest five backups stay.
  • If your data cannot be opened, Deepkoi says it is still there and quits without changing it.
  • Start a session on the open task with only a duration to pick.
  • Rename a task from its title, and double-click the sticky bar to open the task in focus.
  • Add an epic from the + next to Epics. A new install starts with no epics.
  • Sessions shorter than a minimum you set in Settings are dropped.
  • Buttons press down and come back when you click them.
  • Fixes: the sticky bar and the Dock icon after starting at login, and the editor placeholder in an empty list.
