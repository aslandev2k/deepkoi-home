Deepkoi 1.1.0

  • Check for Updates in the Deepkoi menu (Option-Command-U) finds a newer version and installs it. Deepkoi connects to the internet only when you click it.
  • Before a new version changes how your data is saved, and right before an update installs, Deepkoi backs up your data and music. The newest five backups stay.
  • If your data cannot be opened, Deepkoi says it is still there and quits without changing it.
  • Start a session on the open task with only a duration to pick, and double-click the sticky bar to open the task in focus.
  • Sessions with less focus than a minimum set in Settings are dropped.
  • A new install starts with no epics: add your own from the + next to Epics.
  • Rename a task from its title. A new task left untitled is dropped when you close its title with esc.
  • Hover and click a day in the Dashboard's Last 7 days.
  • Buttons press down and come back when you click them. Delete from a "…" menu with a single click.
  • Fixes: the sticky bar and the Dock icon after starting at login, the format bar on the last line, and the hint over a list on an empty page.
