Deepkoi 1.3.1

  • A formatting toolbar sits at the bottom of a task's content. Expand it with the tool icon or Format > Show Toolbar (Option-Command-T).
  • Click a row in Today's sessions on the Dashboard to open that session in a popup.
  • Double-click a task in the start-session list to start it.
  • Each epic's own icon shows in the start-session popup and on the sticky bar, in place of the plain dot.
  • Sessions without a note no longer show a "No note" line.
  • Rename is gone from a task's "..." menu: click the title or use File > Rename Task.
